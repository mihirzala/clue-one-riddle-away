How to Play

  • 🧩 10 new riddles every day, getting harder as you go.
  • ❤️ You start with 4 lives. A wrong answer costs 1 life.
  • 💡 Get Hint gives you up to 2 stages of clues, costing round points.
  • 🔍 Reveal Letter shows part of the answer, costing a life (very short answers give a free extra hint instead).
  • 🏦 After each riddle: bank your points and stop, or keep going — a miss costs 75% of your round points.
  • 🏆 Solve all 10 and today's challenge is complete. A new set is waiting tomorrow.
  • ⏳ You get one attempt per day — running out of lives or banking your points ends today's attempt for good, not just the round. Closing the app and coming back resumes exactly where you left off.
